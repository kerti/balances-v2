# Zone: FINANCE

The calculation correctness that *is* the product (ADR-0021's second heavy
zone). The materialized monthly report (ADR-0006) derives net worth, the
comprehensive-income statement (ADR-0008), and the multi-currency conversion
(ADR-0002) from snapshots + transactions. A wrong number here silently misstates
a household's wealth — the failure is invisible until someone reconciles by hand.
The compute core is the pure, DB-free engine `internal/repo/monthly_reports_engine.go`;
its rules are unit-tested without a container.

| INV-FINANCE-25 | A paid-out bond coupon (`coupon_disposition='pays_out'`) is passive *cash*: the engine materializes it as `passive_coupon_cash`, and buildStats adds it to the passive-cash scope (Passive-Income numerator + Fund Resilience draw-offset) **and** subtracts it from own-return `g` — the two-scope split that guards the double-count. | INV-FINANCE-26 | The PDF report's earned-income drill-down decomposes the month's income by source with Active (`salary+business+gift+tax_refund+insurance+other`) + Passive (`rental+pension+interest`) == `earned_income_total` — a single-month, all-income decomposition, **deliberately unlike** the trailing-12 routine-only passive ratio (INV-FINANCE-19/24). Paid-out coupon cash (`passive_coupon_cash`) surfaces as a separate additive line, never folded into the earned-income total or the cash-flow Net | ADR-0048 | High |
| INV-FINANCE-27 | The statistics panel's reproducibility inputs are the exact trailing-12 (routine-income) operands the two flow ratios divide, as per-month averages: `Cash-Flow == (AvgIncome − AvgExpenses)/AvgIncome` and `Passive-Income == AvgPassive/AvgExpenses`. | INV-FINANCE-28 | The seeded **demo** dataset reconciles as one household cash flow: the Everyday Checking account is the cash plug (income in, investment purchases out, payouts/coupons in, debt paid, chosen living expenses out), so the engine's derived Living Expenses come out positive and plausible every non-baseline month rather than an arbitrary residual, and no month books a phantom investment loss from a Buy the snapshot quantity doesn't reflect (the checking series funds every IDR cash flow that INV-FINANCE-06/08 book; USD bank/receivable revaluation is a small tolerated leak). | INV-FINANCE-29 | Investment return **and** closing value each reconcile across both partitions of the Investment group: `Σ investment_return_{low,medium,high} == investment_return_total`, and `Σ investment_value_{low,medium,high} == Σ investment_value_{stock,mutual_fund,bond,gold,time_deposit} == nw_investments`. | INV-FINANCE-30 | A bucket's this-month investment-return **rate** = bucket return ÷ bucket **opening** (prior-month) invested value; it is **undefined** (renders "—", never `÷0` or a misleading `0%`) when the opening base is zero or absent (bucket held its first month, or fully exited). The three rate headlines (total, per-risk, per-subtype) do **not** sum — each divides by its own base — while the underlying amounts reconcile (INV-FINANCE-29). Extends the undefined-state convention (INV-FINANCE-22) | ADR-0048 | High |
| INV-FINANCE-31 | The trailing-12 investment-return rate is the **geometric compound** `Π(1 + rₘ) − 1` over in-window months with a defined base (a month with a zero/absent opening base contributes no factor), **not** the arithmetic mean of the monthly rates — which would overstate a compounding series | ADR-0048 | High |
| INV-FINANCE-32 | `investment_placement` is **new bank-sourced money into investments** — Buy transactions + fresh TD placements, FX-converted — and **excludes** TD rollover funding (`rolled_to_new` principal/interest, internal recycling that never touches the bank; else an `auto_renew` TD reads as a huge recurring placement) and cash `fee` inflows. Rendered as a share of the **opening** invested pool (undefined → "—" on a zero/absent pool); its trailing-12 figure is the **arithmetic average** (`Σplacement / Σopening-pool`; amount `Σ / n`), deliberately **not** the geometric compound of the return rate (INV-FINANCE-31), because placement is a flow, not a compounding growth rate | ADR-0048 | High |
